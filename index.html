MISMADE

You were the Court Necromancer of Murk until your own apprentice burned you, four hundred years ago, while the whole kingdom cheered. Now you are back: a lich with no spells, a leaky crypt, a floating skull for a mentor, and a grudge.

Raise an army, take the world town by town, and save the saint who did it for last.

What you do

  • Raise the dead. Dig up the graveyard, drag the bodies home and bring them back.
  • Build creatures nobody designed. Take bodies apart on the Bone Slab and stitch the pieces back together.
  • Conquer towns. Ring the bells, beat the militia and the captain, and the town is yours.
  • Play together. 1–4 player online co-op, in first or third person.
  • Fight bosses with an army of your own making.
